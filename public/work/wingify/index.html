Problem

Customers needed VWO to work with the tools they already used. Teams migrating from Google Optimize also needed to bring existing experiments into VWO.

I worked on both needs: backend integrations for ongoing workflows and an importer for experiment migration.

Constraints

The work spanned distinct external platforms rather than one customer workflow. I led integration builds and launches for Slack, Marketo, WordPress, Twilio Segment, and RudderStack.

Experiment migration, onboarding, and marketplace distribution were separate pieces of the adoption problem. The Google Optimize importer, Google Tag Manager flow, and Shopify app served those different needs.

Architecture

Alongside the importer, I designed and implemented backend architectures for third-party VWO integrations. The named integrations were separate deliveries from the experiment migration path shown here.

Decisions

The work addressed adoption at different boundaries. The importer supported bringing existing experiments into VWO. Third-party integrations supported ongoing customer workflows. The Google Tag Manager onboarding flow and Shopify app addressed setup and marketplace presence.

Execution

  • Built the Google Optimize experiment importer and backend architectures for third-party integrations.
  • Led builds and launches for Slack, Marketo, WordPress, Twilio Segment, and RudderStack integrations.
  • Built a Google Tag Manager onboarding flow.
  • Built and deployed a Shopify app for VWO.

Impact

The Google Tag Manager onboarding flow generated 45+ leads. The Google Optimize importer helped convert migrating teams into customers, while the Shopify app increased marketplace visibility and improved its user experience.
